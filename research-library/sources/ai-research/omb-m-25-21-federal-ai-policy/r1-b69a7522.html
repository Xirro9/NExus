White House release summary (April 7, 2025) From the official White House release "White House Releases New Policies on Federal Agency AI Use and Procurement" (dated April 7, 2025 on the page), fetched live 2026-11-09: the White House Office of Management and Budget (OMB) released two revised policies on Federal Agency Use of AI and Federal Procurement, delivering on President Trump's decisive Executive Order to remove barriers to American leadership in Artificial Intelligence (AI); the memos were revised at the direction of the Executive Order and in coordination with the Assistant to the President on Science and Technology and the Office of Science and Technology Policy (OSTP). The release frames the guidance as removing "unnecessary bureaucratic restrictions," allowing agencies to be "more efficient and cost-effective," and supporting "a competitive American AI marketplace" (Lynne Parker, Principal Deputy Director, White House OSTP), and cites a "widening gap in adopting AI and modernizing government technology" that the policies address (Greg Barbaccia, Office of the Federal Chief Information Officer). The release links: OMB Memorandum M-25-21, Accelerating Federal Use of AI through Innovation, Governance, and Public Trust; OMB Memorandum M-25-22, Driving Efficient Acquisition of Artificial Intelligence in Government; and a Fact Sheet, "Eliminating Barriers for Federal Artificial Intelligence Use and Procurement." Later AI-related memoranda on the same index: M-26-04 Increasing Public Trust in Artificial Intelligence Through Unbiased AI Principles (December 11, 2025); M-26-10 Reinforcing Transparency, Accountability, and Oversight of Federal Technology (March 31, 2026).
